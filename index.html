How To Play

  1. 1
    READ

    A short Physics question sits at the top of the screen.

  2. 2
    THINK

    Fruits fly up — each one carries a possible answer.

  3. 3
    SLICE

    Swipe straight through the correct fruit. A tap works too.

  4. 4
    STREAK

    Chain correct answers to build a multiplier up to ×2.

✓ Correct Points, a speed bonus, and your streak grows.

✗ Wrong Lose 50 points and a life. The correct fruit is revealed.

⏳ Too slow Lose 25 points and your streak. Only Survival takes a life.

✖ Bomb Dark bombs appear later in a round. Never slice them.

Keyboard: 1 2 3 4 slice fruit left to right. Esc pauses.
